Two halves of the same work

Machine learning on human signals

Speech-based screening for dementia and depression, spiking neural networks and liquid state machines, eye tracking and human–computer interaction. Published in Frontiers in Neuroinformatics and Frontiers in Psychology.

My doctorate, defended summa cum laude at the Polish-Japanese Academy of Information Technology in 2023, built a reproducible simulation pipeline for liquid state machines, with computing time granted by the Hasso-Plattner-Institut in Potsdam.

Governance for systems under audit

Twenty years in the field, fourteen of them leading teams. At Wyden: certification programmes across ISO, ISAE and GDPR, policy governance and internal compliance. At Sportradar: security assessments and regulatory and client audits, with teams in Warsaw, Vienna, Bratislava and London. At Aviva: vulnerability management and penetration testing across the Group Platforms estate. ISO/IEC 27001:2022 Lead Implementer, certified by BSI.

Head of Data Governance & Compliance at Wyden, the global leader in institutional digital-asset trading technology, where data protection runs under two regimes at once: Data Protection Advisor (DPA) under the revised Swiss Federal Act on Data Protection, and Data Protection Officer (DPO) for the Polish entity under the GDPR. Previously Head of Security Advisory at Sportradar, CISO at Technicus and IT Security Manager for Aviva's Group Platforms.
